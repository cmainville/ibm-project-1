RISKS AND COMMITTEE ACTION
08 / 08

Risks and Committee Decision

Defer initiation

Keep IBM on watch. The base valuation and the high causal case do not clear the September 1 price, and important bridge/WACC assumptions remain qualified.

Monitor

  • Software growth and renewal evidence versus the 6% to 4.5% path.
  • Gross margin and SG&A conversion toward 59% and 29%.
  • Cash conversion and debt reduction versus the pro forma.

Positive review condition

  • Two reported quarters support the operating case.
  • Reproducible beta and IBM debt-yield evidence reduce WACC uncertainty.
  • Pension, diluted shares and Financing value reconcile without a material equity deduction.

Reversal triggers

  • Software growth or margin falls below the base path.
  • Debt rises or cash conversion weakens materially.
  • Validated bridge claims reduce value, or price remains above conservative value.
Committee action: no position today. Next review: next reported quarter or earlier if verified bridge/WACC evidence materially changes the conservative valuation range. Sources: IBM filings, IBM investor relations, Treasury, FRED, Damodaran and peer filings, all documented in the evidence ledger.
