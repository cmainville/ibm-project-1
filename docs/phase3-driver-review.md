# Phase 3 Driver Review — Provisional Paths for Review

**Purpose:** review the assumptions behind the provisional base, low, and high screens. All figures are analyst draft assumptions unless explicitly labeled as reported or management guidance. No partner challenge is attributed because no documented partner-review record is in the workspace. The case runs do not constitute approval of the assumptions or investment action.

## Revenue build

FY2026 starts with reported H1 actual segment revenue. H2 uses each segment’s FY2025 H2/H1 revenue ratio; this produces an FY2026 reported-revenue growth result near IBM’s 4%–5% constant-currency guidance but is not a claim that the two measures are identical.

| Segment | H1 2026 actual | FY2026 H2 draft method | FY2027E | FY2028E | FY2029E | FY2030E | Defensible range | Basis / challenge | Evidence that would change it |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| Software | 14,813 | FY2025 H2/H1 seasonality | 6.0% | 5.5% | 5.0% | 4.5% | 3%–8% | H1 grew 7.9% YoY; Q2 grew 5.1%. Path assumes fade, not an acceleration. | Software ARR, Red Hat/Data growth, renewals, or disclosed Confluent contribution. |
| Consulting | 10,599 | FY2025 H2/H1 seasonality | 2.5% | 3.0% | 3.0% | 3.0% | 0%–4% | H1 grew 2.1%; Q2 only 0.2%, so recovery is a load-bearing judgment. | Bookings, utilization, consulting margin, or recurring managed-services indicators. |
| Infrastructure | 7,161 | FY2025 H2/H1 seasonality | 2.0% | 2.0% | 2.0% | 2.0% | -4%–5% | H1 grew 1.9%, while Q2 declined 7.4%; product-cycle and supply timing make the draft growth path uncertain. | IBM Z / Transaction Processing trends, supply conditions, and H2 conversion. |
| Financing | 406 | FY2025 H2/H1 seasonality | 2.0% | 2.0% | 2.0% | 2.0% | -3%–4% | Separate sidecar; revenue is not used in operating FCFF. | Financing receivables, credit losses, funding mix, and return on equity. |
| Other | 100 | FY2025 H2/H1 seasonality | 0.0% | 0.0% | 0.0% | 0.0% | n/m; immaterial | Kept flat after FY2026 for transparency. | A material disclosed recurring component. |

**H2 / residual-FY2026 timing:** no monthly segment cash-flow seasonality is available in the evidence packet. The model therefore uses FY2025 H2/H1 revenue ratios for full FY2026 and a disclosed proportional convention for valuation: 122 of 184 H2 days (66.3%) are after September 1. This is deliberately a provisional allocation and must be stress-tested before Phase 4.
