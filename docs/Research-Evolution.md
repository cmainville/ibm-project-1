# Project 1 — Edition A: IBM

**Prepared for:** Fund Investment Committee  
**Decision:** Whether to allocate further diligence time toward a potential IBM equity investment. This is not yet a buy recommendation or authorization to trade.  
**Company / valuation date:** International Business Machines Corporation (NYSE: IBM); valuation date **September 1, 2026**. The current share price, market capitalization, enterprise value, and trading multiples have not yet been collected, so no valuation conclusion is made in this edition.

## Initial thesis — Watch / defer

IBM appears to have a potentially investable software-led earnings and cash-flow story: FY2025 revenue grew, Software revenue grew faster, and Software ARR increased. But the evidence collected so far does not establish whether those improvements are already reflected in IBM's share price, nor does it establish a satisfactory return after accounting for a meaningful debt balance. The appropriate initial posture is **watch / defer** pending valuation, cash-flow-quality, and leverage work.

## Known evidence

## Consequential assumptions

1. The increase in Software ARR reflects durable recurring demand rather than predominantly acquisition-related growth or a temporary booking effect.
2. IBM can service and refinance its debt without impairing shareholder returns or curtailing necessary investment.
3. Higher-growth software can increase IBM's earnings quality and valuation relevance even while Consulting and Infrastructure remain substantial parts of the business.
4. The FY2025 results are sufficiently representative to serve as a starting point for forward underwriting; this must be tested against FY2026 results and guidance.

## Unknowns that prevent an initiation decision

- Current share price, market capitalization, enterprise value, and the multiples implied by the September 1, 2026 valuation date.
- Consensus revenue, EPS, free-cash-flow expectations, and the gap between those expectations and management guidance.
- A full cash-flow bridge: operating cash flow, capital expenditures, acquisitions, dividends, buybacks, and free cash flow after the chosen definition.
- Debt maturities, interest expense, floating/fixed-rate mix, restricted or overseas cash, and the relationship between operating debt and IBM Financing debt.
- Segment margins, organic versus acquired growth, retention/churn, customer concentration, competitive positioning, and the durability of ARR.
- The key catalyst path and the evidence that would show that the software-mix thesis is failing.

## What the additional research established

**Current operating evidence and the forecast.** Research extended the FY2025 Form 10-K starting point to IBM's June 30, 2026 Form 10-Q and contemporaneous July earnings disclosure. FY2026 uses H1 reported actuals plus H2 forecast. Software growth is modeled at 6.0%, 5.5%, 5.0%, then 4.5% for 2027–2030; Consulting recovery and positive Infrastructure growth are less certain. Operating gross margin rises from 57.2% to 59.0% by 2030, while SG&A falls from 30.8% to 29.0% of operating revenue. These are *analyst forecasts*, not company guidance.

**Integrated valuation, not just multiples.** A connected five-year income statement, balance sheet, and cash-flow model supports an operating FCFF DCF. IBM Financing is excluded from operating cash flow and added once in the common-equity bridge using $1.450bn of reported June book equity. The model separately considers corporate debt, excess cash, minority interests and the declared-but-unpaid September 10 dividend. Oracle and Accenture EV/EBIT and P/E applications are **qualified reference anchors**, not interchangeable peers or a blended target.

**Testing the assumptions.** One-at-a-time operating shocks change intrinsic value by **+$12.20/share** for +1 percentage point of gross margin, **+$8.36** for +2 percentage points of Software growth, and **+$6.14** for a 0.5-point improvement in SG&A/revenue. These shocks have different magnitudes; their ordering is not a standardized ranking of economic importance. A favorable WACC / long-run-growth combination can put value above market, illustrating why the conclusion is conditional rather than certain.

## Consequential repairs and disposition of AI assistance

The research process involved student-directed AI coding and analytical assistance, independent source and arithmetic checks, manual operating tests, and documented corrections. The important revisions were:

1. **Period boundary:** FY2026 balance-sheet cash and equity must roll forward from June 30 using H2 activity only, not a second application of H1. For the September 1 after-close DCF, remaining FY2026 FCFF begins September 2 and uses **121/184** of the H2 forecast, correcting a superseded **122/184** convention. Tests enforce the date and bridge logic.
2. **Equity bridge:** The $1.69 per-share dividend payable September 10 is deducted **once**; Financing operating and debt flows are not mixed into ex-Financing FCFF. A 952.7m H1 diluted-share average is explicitly a proxy, not a September 1 exact count.
3. **AI-output correction:** An earlier AI-assisted timing/valuation output used the incorrect stub convention. The discrepancy was checked against the valuation boundary, corrected in code, and captured in the AI-use record rather than accepted on authority.
4. **Stock-based compensation (SBC):** A subsequent review identified missing SBC treatment in forecast consolidated CFO. On October 14, the student approved **$938.5m H2 2026** and **$1.877bn each year 2027–2030** as provisional SBC assumptions. SBC was added once to CFO and APIC/equity, not added as free operating FCFF. The approved cash-bridge repair moved the **current base value from $181.95 to $182.29** without changing operating EV. Future dilution is not independently modeled.
5. **Historical locked experiment:** On October 10, the original *pre-SBC* workbench recorded a software-growth shift of 0 to +2 percentage points, with a pre-run prediction of higher revenue, EBIT, FCFF and value but no action change. Observed per-share value moved **$181.95 to $190.31** while Watch / Defer remained. Original BEFORE/AFTER text, commit references and screenshots are retained as historical evidence; that result is not restated as a test of the later $182.29 baseline.

## What remains uncertain and what would change the decision

**Material, unresolved cash-conversion qualification.** After the SBC correction, the model's approximately **$10.918bn FY2026 company-style FCF proxy** is **$4.782bn lower** than a roughly $15.7bn management-outlook reference available by the cutoff. Forecast cash taxes, Financing-receivable movements, H2 seasonality, and the non-identical capital-investment definition remain insufficiently reconciled. Consolidated CFO, IBM-defined FCF and unlevered operating FCFF are **different measures**; the model was not forced to meet guidance.

**Valuation qualification.** The 0.70 beta has an opaque third-party methodology; the 5.47% pretax debt cost is a Treasury-plus-single-A-spread proxy rather than an IBM-specific bond yield; pension and Financing values require fuller reconciliation; future SBC dilution and point-in-time diluted shares remain unresolved. With **78.06%** of operating EV arising from terminal value, the DCF is sensitive to steady-state assumptions. The published Week 3 training-case reconciliation now passes all 12 published outputs within tolerance: conventional DCF value is **$27.4974/share at 10% WACC** and **$23.4140 at 11%**. It validates conventional DCF arithmetic only, not IBM-specific timing, Financing treatment, or ROIC-based terminal reinvestment; see `docs/week3-known-answer-reconciliation.md`.

**Committee conditions.** Maintain Watch / Defer until reported software demand, renewal performance, Consulting recovery and gross-margin/SG&A conversion support the forecast, and cash-flow quality and equity-bridge items become independently reproducible. Revisit after two relevant reporting quarters or sooner if credible evidence materially changes value. Re-underwrite downward if growth, conversion or leverage deteriorate, or if validated pension/dilution/financing claims reduce equity value. Initiation requires a **conservatively supported value above the prevailing price**, not merely an optimistic sensitivity cell.

## Bottom line

The software-led IBM opportunity remains worth monitoring, but the research did not establish an attractive entry price on September 1, 2026. Edition B turns the initial research plan into a reproducible and falsifiable decision process. Watch / Defer is **confirmed by evidence**, not simply repeated from Edition A.
